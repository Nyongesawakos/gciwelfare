GCI Welfare Association

Established to support the welfare of church members through assistance, participation, and collective contribution, especially during times of need and community support.

Our Membership Journey

The association was launched in October 2024 with a registration fee of Ksh 200. By April 2025, over 105 members had already joined the welfare initiative.

Membership Policy

Membership is voluntary and open exclusively to members of GCI Thindigua Church who are committed to unity, support, and welfare participation.
